# Photos wanted: seed-mc4-leads

Places where a real photograph would serve the customer better than the drawing. Each photo is to be taken
by a person, of real parts, with no brand shown unless the pack is for that brand. Three openly licensed
photos are already in `images/third-party/` (listed in `manifest.json`); the items below are still wanted.

- **step-03, same make.** Two matching connector halves side by side, the maker's name readable on both;
  and, apart, two halves of different makes that look alike, so the small differences show.
- **step-04, covered panel.** A panel lying face down on a soft surface, and a panel fully covered by a
  dark blanket, outdoors.
- **step-06, plus to plus.** A panel lead joined to a power station's adapter cable, plus to plus, with
  the + and − marks on both sides readable.
- **step-09, fully locked.** A connector pair fully locked, close up, with no gap; and the same pair part
  way in, with the gap showing.
- **step-10, cable laid well.** A connector pair held up off the ground on a post or frame, the cable
  leaving it straight and curving gently.
- **step-11, device showing solar input.** The display of a power station or charge controller showing
  solar power coming in, in daylight.
- **step-12, release tool.** The release tool for one connector make, placed on the locking tabs.
- **fail-11, connectors on wet ground.** Connectors lying in a puddle, as a picture of what to avoid.
